CANCELLATION AND NON-RENEWAL
============================

Cancellation and non-renewal are different ways an insurance relationship can end or not continue, and the exact rules depend on product and jurisdiction.

CANCELLATION
------------
Cancellation generally ends a policy before its scheduled expiration date, subject to the policy and applicable law.

NON-RENEWAL
-----------
Non-renewal means the insurer or insured does not continue the policy into a new term after the current term ends, subject to notice and legal requirements.

WHY DISTINCTION MATTERS
-----------------------
Timing, notice, refund, underwriting reasons and regulatory requirements can differ.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied NAIC material distinguishes cancellation and non-renewal and discusses insurer responsibilities. Exact requirements must be checked for the applicable jurisdiction.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Using the terms interchangeably or assuming a rule from one jurisdiction applies everywhere.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Cancellation ends coverage before expiration; non-renewal concerns continuation into a subsequent term. Legal rules vary.
