CONTRIBUTION
============

Contribution is a mechanism used when more than one insurance arrangement may respond to the same loss, allowing insurers to share the applicable burden according to governing rules.

CONCEPT
-------
Contribution helps avoid an insured receiving more than the permitted recovery for the same indemnifiable loss while allocating responsibility among insurers.

EXAMPLE
-------
If two policies validly cover the same property loss, the insurers may determine how each should participate.

DISTINCTION
-----------
Contribution is different from subrogation: contribution addresses sharing among insurers, while subrogation generally concerns recovery from a responsible third party.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Confusing contribution with the insured's deductible or assuming every overlapping policy automatically contributes in the same way.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Contribution concerns allocation among insurers when multiple applicable coverages exist.
