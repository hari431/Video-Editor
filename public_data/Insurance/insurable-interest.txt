INSURABLE INTEREST
==================

Insurable interest means the person seeking insurance has a recognized financial or other legally relevant interest in the subject of insurance such that loss would matter to them.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
Insurable interest helps ensure insurance protects against genuine exposure rather than functioning as a wager on someone else's loss.

EXAMPLES
--------
A homeowner has an economic interest in a home. A lender can have an interest in property securing a loan. The exact legal requirements depend on jurisdiction and product.

TIMING
------
The required timing of insurable interest varies by type of insurance and applicable law. Do not assume one rule applies universally.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming any personal connection is automatically an insurable interest or applying one jurisdiction's rule to another without checking the law.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Insurable interest connects the insurance contract to a legitimate exposure to loss; exact legal rules depend on the product and jurisdiction.
