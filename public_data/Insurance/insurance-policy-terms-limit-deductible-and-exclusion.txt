INSURANCE POLICY TERMS: LIMIT, DEDUCTIBLE AND EXCLUSION
=======================================================

Limits, deductibles and exclusions are three policy concepts that strongly influence whether and how much a claim is paid.

LIMIT
-----
A limit is the maximum amount or scope specified for a coverage, subject to the policy's terms.

DEDUCTIBLE
----------
The deductible is the portion of an applicable covered loss generally borne by the insured.

EXCLUSION
---------
An exclusion removes certain causes, property, situations or losses from coverage. Exclusions can be broad or subject to exceptions.

EXAMPLE
-------
A policy may have a 500 deductible and a 100,000 coverage limit, but an excluded cause of loss can still result in no payment even though the limit is high.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Looking only at the limit and ignoring exclusions and conditions.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Coverage is determined by the whole policy structure: insuring agreement, definitions, exclusions, conditions, limits and deductibles.
