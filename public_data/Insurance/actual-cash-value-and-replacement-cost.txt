ACTUAL CASH VALUE AND REPLACEMENT COST
======================================

Actual cash value (ACV) and replacement cost (RC) are different approaches to valuing covered property losses.

ACV
---
ACV commonly reflects replacement cost less applicable depreciation, although exact definitions vary by policy and jurisdiction.

REPLACEMENT COST
----------------
Replacement cost aims to pay the cost to repair or replace with like kind and quality subject to the policy's terms, limits and conditions.

EXAMPLE
-------
If replacing an item costs 2,000 and applicable depreciation is 800, a simplified ACV calculation would be 1,200 before deductible and other policy provisions.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied NAIC home-insurance guide discusses replacement cost, market value and actual cash value. This article independently explains the concepts rather than reproducing the guide.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming ACV always equals market value or assuming replacement cost automatically ignores policy limits.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Valuation basis matters. Always check the policy definition, applicable limits, depreciation rules and deductible.
