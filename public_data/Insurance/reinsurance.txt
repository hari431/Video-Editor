REINSURANCE
===========

Reinsurance is insurance purchased by an insurer to transfer part of its exposure to another insurer or reinsurer.

WHY IT EXISTS
-------------
Reinsurance can help manage catastrophe exposure, capacity, capital needs and portfolio volatility.

CONCEPTUAL EXAMPLE
------------------
An insurer may retain a defined portion of losses and transfer another portion under a reinsurance agreement. The exact structure can be quota share, excess of loss or another arrangement.

NOT THE SAME AS COINSURANCE
---------------------------
Reinsurance is primarily an arrangement between insurance organizations. Coinsurance can describe shared coverage between insurers in other contexts.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Thinking the policyholder automatically deals directly with the reinsurer or assuming all reinsurance structures work the same way.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Reinsurance helps insurers manage their own risk; it usually sits behind the primary insurance relationship.
