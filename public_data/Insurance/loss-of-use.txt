LOSS OF USE
===========

Loss of use coverage can help with additional living expenses when a covered property loss makes the insured home unsuitable for normal occupancy, subject to policy terms.

EXAMPLE
-------
If a covered fire makes a home temporarily uninhabitable, eligible additional costs for temporary accommodation and related living expenses may be covered within the policy's rules.

WHAT IT IS NOT
--------------
It is not a general reimbursement for every inconvenience. The loss must meet the policy's coverage conditions.

CLAIMS HANDLING
---------------
The insurer may evaluate the cause of loss, habitability, reasonable additional expenses, duration and applicable limits.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming every hotel bill is automatically covered or ignoring the requirement that the underlying event be covered.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Loss of use is tied to a covered event and policy-defined additional expenses; documentation is important.
