RENEWAL
=======

A renewal creates or continues a new policy term after the current term, subject to underwriting, product and regulatory rules.

BUSINESS VIEW
-------------
Renewal processing may reassess risk, apply new rates or forms, and determine whether the insurer will continue the relationship.

AUTOMATED PROCESSING
--------------------
Large insurers often process renewals in batches or workflows, while exceptions may require human action.

POLICYCENTER CONNECTION
-----------------------
In current Guidewire Cloud PolicyCenter Cloud API documentation, a renewal is a policy transaction that creates a new term for an existing policy at the end of the latest term. A renewal can be initiated from the policy and may require further actions depending on its state.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating renewal as simply copying the previous policy without considering new term dates, underwriting decisions, rates, forms and changes.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Renewal is a new term decision and transaction, not merely a duplicate of the old policy.
