INSURANCE AND THE PRINCIPLE OF RISK TRANSFER
============================================

Insurance changes who bears defined financial consequences of specified events, but it does not eliminate the underlying physical or operational risk.

TRANSFER
--------
A policy transfers specified financial risk to the insurer in exchange for premium, subject to the contract.

RESIDUAL RISK
-------------
Deductibles, exclusions, limits, uncovered perils and policy conditions mean the insured may still retain financial exposure.

RISK MANAGEMENT
---------------
Insurance is one tool alongside prevention, mitigation, safety controls, diversification and emergency planning.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Thinking insurance removes the need for risk controls or assuming all consequences of an event are transferable.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Insurance transfers defined financial risk; it does not make the underlying hazard disappear.
