UNDERWRITING
============

Underwriting is the process of evaluating risk for acceptance, terms and pricing within an insurer's rules and authority.

INPUTS
------
Underwriting can consider exposure details, prior losses, property characteristics, applicant information, coverage choices and other permitted factors.

OUTPUTS
-------
Possible outcomes include acceptance, rejection, referral, modified terms, different deductibles, limits or pricing.

HUMAN AND AUTOMATED WORK
------------------------
Modern insurance operations can combine rules, data, external services and underwriter judgment. A referral does not necessarily mean a claim or application is declined.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating underwriting as only a price calculation or assuming every underwriting rule is identical across products.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Underwriting is risk selection and terms management, not simply quoting a premium.
