RISK AND UNCERTAINTY
====================

Risk is the possibility of an uncertain adverse outcome. Insurance focuses on risks that can be defined, measured and managed within an insurance arrangement.

RISK CONCEPTS
-------------
Pure risk involves the possibility of loss without a corresponding speculative gain and is commonly associated with insurance. Speculative risk includes potential gain or loss and is generally treated differently.

EXAMPLES
--------
A house may be damaged by fire; a driver may cause an accident; a person may experience a premature death. The probability and severity of outcomes influence underwriting.

INSURANCE VIEW
--------------
Insurers group similar exposures, estimate expected losses, add expenses and capital considerations, and establish prices and terms.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Using risk and peril as synonyms, assuming every uncertainty is insurable, and focusing only on probability while ignoring severity.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Insurance decisions consider both likelihood and potential severity, along with whether the exposure fits the insurer's risk appetite and policy structure.
