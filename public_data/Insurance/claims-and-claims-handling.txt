CLAIMS AND CLAIMS HANDLING
==========================

A claim is a request for policy benefits following a loss or event. Claims handling determines whether and how the policy responds.

TYPICAL FLOW
------------
A simplified flow is notice/FNOL, coverage and policy verification, investigation, evaluation, reserve/payment decisions, settlement and closure. Actual workflows vary by product and jurisdiction.

EVIDENCE
--------
Claims may use statements, photographs, estimates, reports, invoices, inspections and other evidence.

ADJUSTER ROLE
-------------
An adjuster or claims professional evaluates facts, policy provisions and damages and communicates with involved parties.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied ClaimCenter reference material covers concepts such as FNOL, exposures, reserves, payments, recoveries, activities and catastrophe handling. This article explains the concepts independently.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Confusing an incident with a covered loss, treating a reserve as a payment, or assuming an initial estimate is always final.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Claims handling combines policy interpretation, investigation, financial controls and communication.
