HOME INSURANCE COVERAGES
========================

Home insurance can combine several coverage areas, commonly including the dwelling, other structures, personal property, loss of use, personal liability and medical payments, depending on the policy form.

COVERAGE STRUCTURE
------------------
Each coverage protects a different exposure. Limits, deductibles, exclusions and conditions determine the actual response.

OPTIONAL COVERAGE
-----------------
The supplied NAIC guide discusses options such as flood, earthquake, sewer/drain backup, scheduled personal property and other endorsements. Availability varies by market and policy.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE
-----------------
A detached garage may fall under other-structures coverage, while temporary accommodation after a covered loss may relate to loss of use.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming every disaster is covered by a standard homeowners policy or confusing the building limit with personal-property limits.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Home insurance is a bundle of distinct coverages. Understanding the exposure-to-coverage mapping is more useful than memorizing labels alone.
