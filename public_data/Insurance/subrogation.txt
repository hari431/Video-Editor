SUBROGATION
===========

Subrogation allows an insurer, after paying a covered loss where the principle applies, to pursue recovery from a responsible third party to the extent permitted by the policy and law.

EXAMPLE
-------
If an insurer pays for damage caused by another responsible party, the insurer may seek recovery from that party or its insurer, subject to applicable rules.

WHY IT MATTERS
--------------
Subrogation can reduce the insurer's ultimate loss and help place responsibility on the party that caused the damage.

PROCESS
-------
Claims teams may identify responsible parties, preserve evidence, coordinate with counsel or recovery specialists, and track recovered amounts.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating subrogation as automatic in every claim or assuming it overrides contractual or legal limitations.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Subrogation connects claim payment with recovery from responsible third parties where permitted.
