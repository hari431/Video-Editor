INSURANCE AGENT AND PRODUCER CONCEPTS
=====================================

Agents and producers can act as distribution and advisory participants in insurance transactions, subject to licensing, appointments, authority and jurisdiction-specific rules.

ROLE
----
A producer may collect customer information, present products, facilitate applications, communicate with insurers and perform other permitted activities.

AUTHORITY
---------
Authority is not unlimited. Binding authority, underwriting authority and other permissions depend on insurer rules, licenses and configuration.

GUIDEWIRE CONNECTION
--------------------
Policy administration systems often represent producers, producer organizations, licensing information and roles because distribution affects transaction processing.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming every producer can bind every product or approve underwriting issues.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Producer responsibilities and authority are governed by product, insurer, licensing and jurisdiction.
