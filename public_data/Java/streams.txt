STREAMS
=======

Streams provide a declarative way to process sequences of values through operations such as filter, map, sorted and reduce.

PIPELINE
--------
A stream pipeline usually has a source, zero or more intermediate operations, and a terminal operation. Intermediate operations are generally lazy.

EXAMPLE
-------
List<String> names = users.stream()
    .filter(User::isActive)
    .map(User::getName)
    .sorted()
    .toList();

WHEN TO USE
-----------
Streams are excellent for transformations and filtering where the pipeline reads naturally. A traditional loop may be clearer when the algorithm is stateful or involves complex control flow.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Overly long pipelines, side effects inside stream operations, accidental repeated traversal, and using parallel streams without understanding workload and cost.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Use streams for clear data transformations; do not sacrifice readability or introduce side effects merely to use a stream.
