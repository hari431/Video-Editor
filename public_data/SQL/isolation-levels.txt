ISOLATION LEVELS
================

Isolation levels define how concurrent transactions may observe one another's work. Different databases implement and name details differently.

TYPICAL PHENOMENA
-----------------
Discussions often include dirty reads, non-repeatable reads and phantom reads. A stronger isolation level can reduce anomalies but may increase contention or reduce concurrency.

CHOICE
------
Use the weakest isolation level that correctly supports the business requirement, but do not choose solely for performance without understanding correctness.

EXAMPLE
-------
A financial operation that requires a stable view or serialization may need stronger guarantees than a simple reporting query.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming the database default is universally correct, confusing snapshot behavior with serializable behavior, and ignoring application retry requirements.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Isolation is a correctness decision. Learn the behavior of the specific database engine rather than relying on generic slogans.
