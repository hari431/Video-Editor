CLAIMCENTER RECOVERIES AND SUBROGATION
======================================

Recoveries are amounts the insurer expects to receive from other parties after paying or reserving for a claim.

EXAMPLES
--------
Recovery can arise through subrogation, salvage, another insurer or other permitted sources.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied ClaimCenter material discusses recoveries, pending-transfer status and recoding corrections.

FINANCIAL RELATIONSHIP
----------------------
A recovery can reduce the net cost of a claim, but it is not the same thing as the original claim payment.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating an expected recovery as guaranteed cash or confusing recovery with reserve adjustment.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Recoveries represent money coming back to the insurer or reducing net loss through defined recovery processes.
