CLAIMCENTER EXPOSURES AND COVERAGES
===================================

An exposure represents a specific aspect of a claim for which the insurer may have an obligation under a coverage.

RELATIONSHIP
------------
A single claim can have multiple exposures. Each exposure can be associated with coverage and financial handling.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied ClaimCenter material describes reserve lines as combining exposure/coverage with cost type and category.

EXAMPLE
-------
An auto accident claim might have separate exposures for vehicle damage and bodily injury depending on the policy and facts.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating the whole claim as one financial exposure or assuming every incident automatically creates an exposure.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Exposures provide a more granular way to manage coverage and financial responsibility within a claim.
