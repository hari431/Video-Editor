POLICYCENTER FORMS
==================

Forms are policy documents or form records associated with coverage, product, jurisdiction or transaction rules. They can communicate contractual terms and endorsements.

TRANSACTION RELATIONSHIP
------------------------
Forms can be inferred or selected based on policy characteristics and transaction context. They are part of the policy's contractual/document package.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material notes that forms are inferred when a transaction is bound and that forms are not simply arbitrary content added outside the transaction.

CLOUD PERSPECTIVE
-----------------
Current PolicyCenter API references expose policy forms as a resource area.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating forms as ordinary UI text or assuming a form can be attached without considering transaction rules.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Forms are part of the insurance contract/document process and are closely tied to product and transaction context.
