CLAIMCENTER CATASTROPHES
========================

A catastrophe is an event that causes many related claims, such as a major storm, wildfire or earthquake.

OPERATIONAL IMPACT
------------------
Catastrophes can create high claim volume, geographic concentration, resource pressure and special handling needs.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied ClaimCenter material describes catastrophe relationships and a one-to-many relationship between catastrophe events and claims.

PRACTICAL VALUE
---------------
Catastrophe identifiers help reporting, assignment, workload management and aggregate-loss analysis.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating catastrophe as merely a label or assuming every large claim is a catastrophe.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Catastrophe management helps insurers coordinate many related claims generated by a common event.
