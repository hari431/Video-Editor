POLICYCENTER RENEWALS
=====================

A renewal creates a new term for an existing policy at the end of the latest term.

CURRENT CLOUD BEHAVIOR
----------------------
Current Guidewire Cloud documentation describes renewal as a policy transaction initiated from the policy. Some renewals may proceed automatically, while others require modification, requoting or underwriting action.

PRE-RENEWAL DIRECTION
---------------------
The current documentation describes pre-renewal directions as information attached to a policy that can influence how an automatically initiated renewal is handled.

OUTCOMES
--------
A renewal can be completed as a renewal, marked non-renewing, marked not taken, or withdrawn depending on the transaction state and business outcome.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming renewal always copies the prior term unchanged or assuming every renewal needs manual work.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Renewal is a new-term transaction with automated and exception paths.
