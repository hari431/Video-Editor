POLICYCENTER AUTHORITY AND UNDERWRITING ISSUES
==============================================

Authority determines what a user or role is permitted to approve or perform. Underwriting issues identify conditions that may require review or resolution.

SEPARATION OF CONCERNS
----------------------
A system can identify an underwriting issue and then route it to someone with appropriate authority. Assignment and authority are related but different.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material states that producers cannot approve underwriting issues and describes multiple authority profiles per user.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE
-----------------
A risk outside a producer's authority can be referred to an underwriter whose authority profile permits the decision.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming a user who can see an issue can automatically approve it.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Underwriting issues create decision points; authority controls who can make those decisions.
