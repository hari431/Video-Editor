POLICYCENTER POLICY JOBS
========================

In PolicyCenter, a job represents a policy transaction such as a submission, renewal, policy change, cancellation, reinstatement or rewrite.

TRANSACTION MODEL
-----------------
Current Cloud API documentation describes policy transactions as Job resources. The job carries the transaction's lifecycle and policy-specific work.

WHY JOBS MATTER
---------------
A policy cannot simply be overwritten because insurers need effective dates, history, rating, underwriting decisions, documents and auditability.

TYPICAL LIFECYCLE
-----------------
A transaction may move through draft, quote, bind/issue, withdrawal or other outcomes depending on job type and configuration.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Thinking a job is the same thing as a policy, or assuming every job type has exactly the same lifecycle.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
A job represents work on a policy. The exact states and actions depend on the transaction type and configuration.
