POLICYCENTER REWRITES
=====================

A rewrite is used to replace or restructure an existing policy through a defined policy transaction, often when normal policy-change processing is not appropriate.

BUSINESS PURPOSE
----------------
A rewrite can address situations where the policy needs to be recreated under changed circumstances while preserving a relationship to the original policy history.

VARIANTS
--------
Current Cloud API documentation lists rewrites and rewrite-new-account jobs among PolicyCenter job types.

CAREFUL HANDLING
----------------
Rewrite behavior is product- and configuration-sensitive. Effective dates, producer information, account relationships and policy history must be understood.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Using rewrite for every major change or treating it as a simple copy operation.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Rewrite is a specialized policy transaction; use it when the business process calls for replacement rather than an ordinary policy change.
