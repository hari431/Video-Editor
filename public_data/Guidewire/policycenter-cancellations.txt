POLICYCENTER CANCELLATIONS
==========================

A cancellation is a policy transaction that terminates an existing policy before its scheduled expiration date.

CURRENT CLOUD API
-----------------
Current Guidewire Cloud documentation describes cancellation as a job type and exposes operations to initiate and complete cancellation transactions.

BUSINESS CONSIDERATIONS
-----------------------
Cancellation can involve a reason, source, effective date, notice requirements, refund calculations and downstream billing/document effects.

WORKFLOW
--------
Depending on the process, cancellation can be bound immediately or scheduled for a future date.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Confusing cancellation with withdrawal of an in-progress job or with non-renewal at term end.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Cancellation ends an existing policy before expiration; it is distinct from non-renewal and job withdrawal.
