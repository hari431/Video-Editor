POLICYCENTER REINSTATEMENTS
===========================

A reinstatement restores a previously canceled policy period to active status under applicable business rules.

BUSINESS MEANING
----------------
Reinstatement is not the creation of a completely unrelated new policy. It reverses or repairs a cancellation outcome within the permitted policy lifecycle.

BILLING RELATIONSHIP
--------------------
Current Guidewire Cloud documentation also describes billing impacts of reinstatement, including charges associated with restoring the canceled portion of coverage.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material identifies reinstatement as a transaction and notes that it can participate in out-of-sequence situations in older training material.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating reinstatement as a brand-new submission or ignoring billing and effective-date implications.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Reinstatement restores coverage after cancellation according to configured rules and may require coordinated billing effects.
