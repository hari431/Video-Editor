GUIDEWIRE POLICY HISTORY AND TRANSACTION THINKING
=================================================

Insurance policy systems need history because a policy is not a single static record; it evolves through effective-dated transactions.

TEMPORAL MODEL
--------------
A policy can have terms and transactions with effective dates. Historical reconstruction requires knowing what transaction was effective at a particular point in time.

WHY IT MATTERS
--------------
Claims, billing, audits, renewals and regulatory inquiries may all depend on historical policy state.

BRANCH AND SLICE MODEL
----------------------
Guidewire implements history with Branch and Slice. A Policy has many Branches (one per Job). Each Branch contains many Slices (time segments). A Slice is effective from `SliceDate` until the next SliceDate or PeriodEnd. Example timeline:

Branch 1 (Submission)         [2026-01-01 ------------------- 2026-07-01 End)
Branch 2 (PolicyChange 06-15) [2026-01-01 ---- 2026-06-15 ---- 2026-07-01)
Bound Branch is the winning branch per effective date after preemption.

Querying historical state requires specifying AsOfDate:

uses gw.api.database.Query

var hist = Query.make(entity.PolicyPeriod)
  .compare(PolicyPeriod#PolicyNumber, Equals, "12345")
  .compare(PolicyPeriod#AsOfDate, Equals, lossDate)
  .select()

Upstream systems that read `PolicyPeriod` without `AsOfDate` often retrace the wrong deductible.

GOSU TIME-AWARE ACCESS
----------------------
Period methods encapsulate date arithmetic:

var coverageAtLoss = period.getCoverageAtDate("CollisionCov", lossDate)
var isEffective = period.isEffectiveAt(lossDate)

Avoid manual `if (effective <= date && date < expiration)` across slices; use the platform's slice accessors which respect branch preemption.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material discusses new versions, out-of-sequence transactions, preemption, rewrites and transaction history.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Looking only at the latest policy values when investigating a historical event. Also selecting `Policy` without a Branch/Slice filter and therefore reporting the most recent terms instead of the terms effective at loss.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Effective-dated transaction history is fundamental to understanding insurance policy administration.
