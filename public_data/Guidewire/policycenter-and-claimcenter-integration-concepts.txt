POLICYCENTER AND CLAIMCENTER INTEGRATION CONCEPTS
=================================================

Policy administration and claims systems meet when a claim needs reliable policy and coverage information.

BUSINESS NEED
-------------
Claims need to know which policy, term, coverages and insured relationships apply to a loss.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material notes integration with ClaimCenter, while the ClaimCenter material describes policy verification and claim-side handling.

PRACTICAL DESIGN
----------------
Integrations should account for effective dates, policy status, coverage applicability and changes rather than relying on a single static policy snapshot.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming the current policy state always represents the state on the date of loss.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Claims and policy systems must reason about coverage in the correct historical and effective-date context.
