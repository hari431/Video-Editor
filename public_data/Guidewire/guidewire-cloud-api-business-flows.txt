GUIDEWIRE CLOUD API BUSINESS FLOWS
==================================

Business flows are API operations that represent meaningful insurance processes rather than exposing database mechanics.

EXAMPLES
--------
Current PolicyCenter Cloud API documentation includes business flows for job types such as submissions, renewals, audits, policy changes, cancellations, reinstatements and rewrites.

WHY BUSINESS FLOWS
------------------
A business operation may require validation, workflow, rating, authorization and downstream processing. One domain operation can therefore involve many internal actions.

PRACTICAL APPROACH
------------------
Model the caller's intent first: 'renew this policy', 'cancel this policy', or 'complete this job'. Then use the supported flow.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Building integrations around internal database assumptions or treating an individual resource update as equivalent to completing a business transaction.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Business-flow APIs preserve the meaning of the insurance operation at the integration boundary.
