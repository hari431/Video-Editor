POLICYCENTER ACTIVITIES
=======================

Activities represent work that needs attention by users or groups, such as reviewing an underwriting issue or completing an operational task.

ASSIGNMENT
----------
Activities can have owners, groups, priorities, due dates and escalation behavior depending on configuration.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material discusses activities, activity patterns, ownership, groups and archived activity search.

OPERATIONAL VALUE
-----------------
Activities connect automated processing with human work. A transaction can continue automatically until a business rule creates an activity requiring attention.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating activities as generic notifications, ignoring ownership, or assuming every activity can be skipped.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Activities are work items, not just messages. Ownership and lifecycle are central to operational processing.
