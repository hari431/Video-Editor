CLAIMCENTER RESERVING ENGINE & DATA MODELS
==========================================

Reserves represent the estimated financial liability set aside by an insurance carrier to cover the future cost of open claims. In Guidewire ClaimCenter, reserves are not merely passive data fields; they are handled by a server-side financial engine that tracks transactions, enforces validation rules, and dynamically calculates financial balances.

PURPOSE & ARCHITECTURAL PATHWAYS
--------------------------------
The primary purpose of reserving is to ensure the insurer remains solvent and has sufficient liquidity to pay future claims. ClaimCenter establishes a structured financial architecture designed to enforce financial control. Every change to a claim's expected cost must be submitted as a formal transaction, which is processed through a strict pipeline consisting of validation rules, authority limit checks, and ledger postings.

FINANCIAL TRANSACTION TYPES IN GOSU
-----------------------------------
All financial activities on a claim inherit from the base `Transaction` entity. In Gosu, there are four fundamental transaction subclasses:
1. Reserve: Establishes or adjusts the estimated remaining liability for a specific Exposure, Cost Type, and Cost Category.
2. Payment: Represents an actual cash disbursement to a claimant, vendor, or attorney.
3. Recovery: Represents funds received by the insurer (e.g. through subrogation or salvage), which offset incurred losses.
4. RecoveryReserve: Establishes estimated future recoveries.

A unique combination of **Exposure + Cost Type + Cost Category** is referred to as a **Reserve Line**. 
- Cost Type: High-level buckets such as Claim Cost, Expense - AO (Adjusting & Operational), and Expense - UL (Unallocated Loss Adjustment).
- Cost Category: Granular categorizations such as Body Injury, Property Damage, Legal Fees, or Medical Costs.

THE RESERVING LIFECYCLE & FINANCIAL BALANCES
--------------------------------------------
Reserves do not exist as static fields on the Claim or Exposure tables. Instead, the financial engine dynamically aggregates transactions to calculate real-time balances using pre-defined formulas (Calculations).

```
   [Initial Reserve Transaction]  ---> Available Reserves = $1,000
                 |
                 v
   [Payment Transaction Issued]  ---> Paid-to-Date = $400
                 |
                 v
   [Automatic Reserve Offset]    ---> Available Reserves drops to $600
                                 ---> Total Incurred = $1,000 (Paid + Reserves)
```

- Available Reserves: The remaining estimated liability. When a payment is made, ClaimCenter can automatically decrease (offset) Available Reserves by the payment amount to ensure that the total incurred cost remains stable unless the adjuster explicitly overrides it.
- Total Incurred: Calculated as `Paid-to-Date + Available Reserves - Recoveries`. This is the true metric used to analyze the total financial impact of a claim.

COMMON MISTAKES & GOSU CODE PITFALLS
------------------------------------
1. Direct Database Manipulation: Attempting to update `AvailableReserves` fields via raw SQL. Because financials rely on double-entry transaction ledgers, modifying fields directly bypasses validation, breaks integrity, and will corrupt the Claim Financials table. Always use the Gosu Financials API:
```gosu
// Creating a Reserve Transaction in Gosu
uses gw.api.financials.FinancialsCalculationUtil

var reserve = claim.createReserve(exposure, CostType.TC_CLAIMCOST, CostCategory.TC_BODYINJURY)
reserve.setAmount(1500.00)
reserve.Comments = "Adjusting body injury reserve based on medical reports"
```
2. Reserving Without Exposure: Reserving all liabilities directly on the "Claim-level" (where exposure is null) even when specific exposures have been created. This degrades analytical granularity and hinders accurate reinsurance reporting.
3. Ignoring Authority Limits: Creating reserve transactions in background batch processes without assigning an authorized user. If the reserve exceeds the system user's authority limit, the transaction will get stuck in "Submitting" or "Pending Approval" state indefinitely.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
- A Reserve is an accounting estimate; a Payment is a cash disbursement.
- Reserves must be allocated to distinct Reserve Lines (Exposure, Cost Type, Cost Category) to maintain reporting accuracy.
- Modifying financial data in ClaimCenter must always occur through Gosu transactions, never via direct column updates.
