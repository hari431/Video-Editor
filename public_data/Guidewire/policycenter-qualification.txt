POLICYCENTER QUALIFICATION
==========================

Qualification is the early evaluation of whether a submission meets configured eligibility or underwriting criteria before proceeding through the full transaction.

PURPOSE
-------
Qualification questions and rules can prevent ineligible risks from moving deeper into the process.

BUSINESS VALUE
--------------
Early qualification reduces wasted rating and underwriting effort and can provide a consistent experience.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material describes qualification questions as underwriting-related questions and identifies qualification as an early submission step.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming qualification is identical to final underwriting. Qualification can be an early gate while later underwriting still evaluates the risk.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Qualification is an early decision point; it is not necessarily the final underwriting decision.
