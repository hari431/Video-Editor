POLICYCENTER BINDING AND ISSUANCE
=================================

Binding is the point at which the insurer commits the transaction as coverage according to the applicable business process. Issuance produces the policy artifacts and downstream effects required by the implementation.

BUSINESS MEANING
----------------
Binding is more than a button: required data, underwriting issues, payment requirements, forms, effective dates and integration outcomes may all matter.

CLOUD API
---------
Current Cloud API business flows commonly use a bind-and-issue operation for jobs when immediate completion is appropriate.

DOWNSTREAM IMPACT
-----------------
Binding can trigger billing instructions, documents, messages, history and other integrations depending on configuration.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating quote as binding or assuming issuance is merely PDF generation.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Binding commits the insurance transaction; issuance and downstream processing can involve multiple coordinated effects.
