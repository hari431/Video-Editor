POLICYCENTER SUBMISSION
=======================

A submission is the transaction used to create a new policy relationship for an applicant.

BUSINESS FLOW
-------------
A submission gathers applicant, risk, product, coverage and other information, applies qualification and underwriting rules, obtains a quote when appropriate, and may eventually bind and issue.

WORKFLOW THINKING
-----------------
The important concept is not just screens but the business state transitions: collect information, validate, qualify, rate, underwrite, quote, bind and issue.

SUBMISSION STATES AND ACTIVITIES
--------------------------------
A Submission Job tracks state: Draft → Quoted → Bound → Issued (or Declined/Closed). Quoting creates a bound Period snapshot; binding commits that branch. Activities drive the workflow — e.g., UW Approval blocks binding until an underwriter with `AuthorityProfile` clears the `UWIssue`. Closing a submission without binding does not create a Policy instance.

GOSU CREATION PATTERN
---------------------
Submissions are often created from an Account (Gosu sketch):

var account = Query.make(entity.Account)
  .compare(Account#AccountNumber, Equals, "10001234").select().first()
Transaction.runWithNewBundle(\bundle -> {
  var submission = new SubmissionBuilder(account, Date.Today, productCode)
    .withEffectiveDate(effectiveDate)
    .create(bundle)
  // Add risk: submission.addPolicyLine("PersonalAutoLine")
})

Real code adds `PolicyAddress`, `Driver`, `Vehicle` entities to the Period's line before quoting; omitting required `Jurisdiction` validation fails at `Quote` with `UWIssue`.

CLOUD API
---------
Current PolicyCenter Cloud API documentation treats submissions as one of the job types exposed through business flows. The exact endpoints and generated line-of-business resources depend on the implementation. Example after Job creation:

POST /job/v1/jobs/{jobId}/quote  → 422 if missing driver DOB; 200 returns `premium` and `underwritingIssues`
POST /job/v1/jobs/{jobId}/bind   → creates `Policy` resource linked to `PolicyPeriod`

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Thinking submission and policy are synonyms. A submission is work toward a policy; after binding, the resulting policy becomes the durable insurance contract for the term. Also querying `Policy` before `Bind` and expecting a PolicyNumber, or attempting to `Bind` while `UWIssue` blocks remain open.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
A submission is the acquisition transaction that can lead to a bound policy.
