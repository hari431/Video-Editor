POLICYCENTER DOCUMENTS AND NOTES
================================

Documents and notes capture different kinds of information around policies and transactions.

DOCUMENTS
---------
Documents can include generated policy artifacts, correspondence or external documents depending on configuration.

NOTES
-----
Notes are generally contextual information recorded by users or processes. They are not interchangeable with contractual policy forms.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied material describes documents and notes associated with accounts, policies and transactions and discusses external document integration.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming a note changes contractual coverage or treating every document as a policy form.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Documents communicate or store artifacts; notes capture contextual information; neither should be assumed to change policy terms by itself.
