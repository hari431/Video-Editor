POLICYCENTER AUDITS
===================

An audit reviews policy exposure or premium-related information after or during a policy term, especially for products where final premium depends on actual exposure.

BUSINESS PURPOSE
----------------
Commercial policies may use estimated exposure at inception and reconcile to actual exposure later.

AUDIT CONCEPTS
--------------
An audit can collect actual payroll, sales, units or other rating exposure depending on the line of business.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material identifies audit as a transaction/subtype and references scheduled items and audit tasks.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming every line of business uses the same audit method or treating audit as a simple claims review.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Audits reconcile or validate exposure information when policy pricing depends on information that may change during the term.
