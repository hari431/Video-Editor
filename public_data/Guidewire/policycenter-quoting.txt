POLICYCENTER QUOTING
====================

Quoting calculates a price for a policy transaction based on the information, product configuration and rating logic applicable to the risk.

CONCEPT
-------
A quote is an outcome of rating and transaction information. Missing required rating data can prevent quoting.

CURRENT CLOUD API
-----------------
Current documentation describes a quote operation on jobs and additional quoting capabilities such as payment information, multiple side-by-side quotes and rating overrides in supported contexts.

BUSINESS EXAMPLE
----------------
A personal auto submission with a covered vehicle but missing required driver information may fail to rate because the risk is incomplete.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating quote as bind, assuming every quote is final, or changing rating inputs without understanding the resulting transaction state.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
A quote is a calculated price for a transaction at a point in its lifecycle; it is not the same as binding.
