POLICYCENTER POLICY HOLDS
=========================

A policy hold is a mechanism that can prevent certain processing or changes for a period or under a defined business condition.

BUSINESS USE
------------
A hold can be used when an insurer needs to pause processing for a region, policy population or operational reason.

REFERENCE GROUNDING
-------------------
The supplied PolicyCenter material describes policy hold as a feature that can prevent creation or changes in a region for a period and associates it with underwriting/business issues.

DESIGN CONSIDERATION
--------------------
A hold should be visible and explainable so users understand why normal processing is blocked.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Treating a hold as a system outage or bypassing it without understanding the business reason.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
A policy hold is an intentional business control, not simply an application failure.
