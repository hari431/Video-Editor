POLICYCENTER AND BILLINGCENTER INTEGRATION CONCEPTS
===================================================

Policy administration and billing are separate business domains that must coordinate when policy transactions change financial obligations.

BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP
---------------------
A policy transaction can change premium, charges, invoices or payment requirements. The policy system records insurance terms; the billing system manages receivables and billing processes.

CURRENT CLOUD EVIDENCE
----------------------
Guidewire Cloud documentation describes billing impacts for policy changes and reinstatements, including creation or adjustment of invoice items in BillingCenter.

INTEGRATION THINKING
--------------------
The important question is not just 'did the policy change?' but 'what financial instruction should the billing system receive?'.

COMMON MISTAKES
---------------
Assuming policy premium and billed amount are always the same or treating billing as a UI concern only.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
Policy and billing are distinct domains connected through financial instructions and transaction outcomes.
